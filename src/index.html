Kazakhstan

Data and methods

  • Population, urban share and population change come from Kazakhstan’s Bureau of National Statistics as of 1 October 2026. Change and net migration cover January through September. Download the statistical workbook
  • Gross regional product and industrial gross value added cover 2025 in current tenge. Industry colors compare mining and manufacturing as shares of industrial value added. Regional accounts · Industry workbook
  • Hex population and density use WorldPop Global2 R2025A v1 (Bondarenko et al., University of Southampton, CC BY 4.0). The Kazakhstan raster for 2025 contains modeled people per pixel at 30 arc-seconds (about 1 km at the equator). Each pixel counts towards the hex containing its centre. Density divides that sum by the entire hex area, including any water or land outside Kazakhstan. Hexes with no valid pixels are shown as unavailable.
  • Settlement populations use the Bureau’s 1 January 2025 workbook where matched. Astana, Almaty and Shymkent use the official October 2026 figures. Locations, aliases and any fallback population estimates come from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Industry statistics on city profiles apply to the named administrative division.
  • Boundaries use simplified OpenStreetMap data (ODbL) via Toponym KZ. Water and rivers use Natural Earth cartography and may show historical shorelines.
  • Elevation is sampled from Mapzen Terrain Tiles (USGS GMTED2010 and SRTM among the source datasets). The relief view combines elevation with land cover. The hex inspection uses coarse samples of the ESA WorldCover 2021 color map.
  • Land cover © ESA WorldCover project 2021. Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2021) processed by the ESA WorldCover consortium. ESA WorldCover
  • Economic districts compare each activity’s share of a division’s gross regional product with its national share. Cards show value added, and map positions are schematic. Value added by activity · Labour force survey
  • Rail geometry comes from Natural Earth. The Aktau port marker uses coordinates from Kazakhstan’s published port boundary. Scheduled airport locations come from OurAirports. Heritage markers are selected UNESCO-listed sites and components. Power sites use the WRI Global Power Plant Database 2021 release (CC BY 4.0). MW describes archived nameplate capacity.
